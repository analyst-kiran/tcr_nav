Live data, signals & diagnostics (NAV button)

Live data. Daily AMFI NAVs (Regular plan, Growth option) replace each fund's NAV, trailing and calendar returns before scoring, using the same rules as the monthly files (reproduced exactly for 99–100% of funds). Benchmarks are pinned to the exact levels implied by your files and rolled forward with ETFs that track the same index. Risk ratios the file leaves blank are calculated with the file's own method and risk-free rate. The As of date rebuilds every fund and benchmark for any past day; fund age is measured at that date. Corpus, ratings and categories always come from the current files.

Signals (🚀 Signals & backtest). Built from TCR's own score at past month-ends, and each one must also be confirmed by the fund's returns. Missing data never qualifies a fund.

Fund drawer. Score history: score now, 1-year and all-time average, deviation, rank, and which pillar moved it (pillar change × TCR weight). Fund intelligence: rolling 1Y/3Y returns at monthly entry points (median, worst, % positive, % beating the benchmark), pain index (average fall below the running high over 3 years), down months in which it fell less than the benchmark, 3-year CAGR with its 10 best days removed, next to the benchmark's (a diagnostic, not an investable return), style from NAV vs reported portfolio and SEBI minimums, manager history, and behaviour tags (Consistent compounder, Defensive, High beta, Return concentrated = relies on its best days more than its benchmark, Recovery specialist, Inconsistent, Style drift). Corrections & recoveries: every fall of 10% or more and its recovery.

Backtest. At every past month-end: TCR's top quarter vs the category average over the next 12 months, how often it caught the future top quarter (random = 25%), in rising vs falling markets and by year, and whether a change in score leads returns. It only includes funds alive today, so it flatters the bottom of each category.
